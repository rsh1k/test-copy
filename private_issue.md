## Understanding Risk Metrics

### EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System)
EPSS is a probability score (0-100%) that estimates the likelihood of a vulnerability being exploited in the wild within the next 30 days. It combines threat intelligence, public exploit availability, and vulnerability characteristics. Scores above 10% indicate elevated exploitation risk, while scores above 70% suggest active exploitation campaigns. For 2025 CVEs, EPSS scores are marked as "TBD" as they require time to accumulate exploitation data.

### CISA KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities)
CISA maintains a catalog of vulnerabilities actively exploited in the wild that pose significant risks to federal enterprises. Inclusion in the KEV catalog indicates confirmed exploitation and mandates patching timelines for government agencies. While none of these CVEs currently appear in the KEV, this status can change rapidly. Organizations should treat KEV-listed vulnerabilities as critical priorities regardless of CVSS scores.
