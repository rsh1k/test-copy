**EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System):** A data-driven probability score (0-1) estimating the likelihood that a vulnerability will be exploited in the wild within the next 30 days. Scores are calculated using machine learning models trained on public exploit data, vulnerability characteristics, and threat intelligence. Higher EPSS scores indicate greater probability of active exploitation.

**CISA KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities Catalog):** A authoritative list maintained by the U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency of vulnerabilities actively exploited in the wild. Inclusion in KEV indicates confirmed exploitation evidence and represents critical risk requiring immediate remediation, especially for federal agencies and critical infrastructure.
