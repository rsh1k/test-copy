## Understanding Risk Metrics

**EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System)**: A probability score (0-100%) indicating the likelihood that a vulnerability will be exploited in the wild within the next 30 days. Scores are based on real-world exploitation data, threat intelligence, and vulnerability characteristics. For example, an EPSS of 0.20% means there's a 0.2% chance of active exploitation in the next month. Higher scores (>1%) indicate significantly elevated risk requiring immediate attention.

**CISA KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities)**: A curated catalog maintained by the U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency listing vulnerabilities with confirmed active exploitation in the wild. Inclusion in CISA KEV indicates that threat actors are actively using the vulnerability in attacks, making remediation critical for federal agencies and high-priority for all organizations. None of the identified CVEs appear in CISA KEV at this time, though the recent 2025 CVEs may be under evaluation.
