## Understanding Risk Metrics

**EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System)**: A probability score (0-100%) that indicates the likelihood of a vulnerability being exploited in the wild within the next 30 days. Scores are calculated using machine learning models that analyze threat intelligence, exploit availability, and historical exploitation patterns. Higher EPSS scores indicate greater urgency for remediation.

**CISA KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities)**: A authoritative catalog maintained by the U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency that lists vulnerabilities actively exploited in the wild. Inclusion in the KEV catalog indicates confirmed exploitation and represents the highest priority vulnerabilities requiring immediate remediation per federal directives.
