## Explanation of Risk Metrics

**EPSS (Exploit Prediction Scoring System)**: A probabilistic score (0-100%) indicating the likelihood that a vulnerability will be exploited in the wild within the next 30 days. Scores are based on observable evidence such as exploit publication, threat intelligence, and historical exploitation patterns. Higher EPSS scores indicate greater exploitation probability.

**CISA KEV (Known Exploited Vulnerabilities)**: A catalog maintained by the U.S. Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency listing vulnerabilities with confirmed active exploitation. Inclusion in KEV indicates the vulnerability is being actively exploited by threat actors and requires immediate remediation.

*Note: "TBD (New)" indicates CVEs from 2025 that may not yet have mature EPSS scoring or KEV classification due to recent disclosure.*
